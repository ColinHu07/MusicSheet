How to label a phrase

Click a numbered marker above the staff to select its note. Enter the syllable you hear, then choose whether it starts a new syllable, repeats the pitch, continues the same note, or moves to a new pitch on the same syllable. Keep a repeated label on each note of a run. A tie across a barline is one continuing note.

Automatic suggestions are shown separately and are never marked correct for you. Use “Uncertain” when needed. The written pitch belongs to the supplied score; you can record a different sung pitch without changing that score. Add a missing sung note if the reference omits it. Mark a phrase reviewed when you have finished listening and checking it.

Edits are saved automatically to local disk while the server is running, with a browser recovery copy and downloadable backups. Annotations are not automatically used for training.
